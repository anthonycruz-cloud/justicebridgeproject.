Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Justice Bridge Project does not provide legal advice or legal representation. We connect people with legal resources and organizations that may be able to help.

Is Justice Bridge Project a law firm?

No. We are a nonprofit initiative focused on legal education, referrals, and expanding access to justice.

How can I volunteer?

Visit our Volunteer page and contact us to learn about current opportunities.

Can I donate?

Yes. Donations will help us grow our programs and expand access to legal resources. Online donations will be available soon.
