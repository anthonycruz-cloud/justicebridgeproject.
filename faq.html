Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Justice Bridge Project and our mission.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Justice Bridge Project does not provide legal advice or legal representation. We connect people with legal resources and educational information.

Are you a law firm?

No. Justice Bridge Project is an independent nonprofit initiative focused on legal education, referrals, and community partnerships.

How can I volunteer?

Visit our Volunteer page or Contact page to learn how you can get involved.

Can I donate?

Yes! Online donations are coming soon. Your support will help us expand educational resources and community outreach.

How do I contact you?

Email us at Anthonycruz@justicebridgeproject.org or call (516) 603-4509.

Who can use Justice Bridge Project?

Anyone looking for legal information, educational resources, or connections to trusted legal aid organizations is welcome to use our resources.

Still Have Questions?

If you can't find the answer you're looking for, please contact us. We'll do our best to point you toward helpful resources.
