Our Mission

Justice Bridge Project exists to connect underserved communities with trusted legal resources, educational information, and nonprofit organizations that help people better understand their rights and available options.

How We Help

We connect individuals with trusted legal resources and educational information to help them take informed next steps.

⚖️ Legal Aid Referrals

Connect with nonprofit legal aid organizations and pro bono programs that may be able to help.

📚 Know Your Rights

Easy-to-understand guides covering common legal topics and everyday legal questions.

🏠 Housing & Family Resources

Learn about housing, family law, and community resources available to you.

🤝 Community Partnerships

We work to build partnerships with nonprofits, schools, and community organizations.

Why We Started

Justice Bridge Project was founded on the belief that understanding your legal rights should never depend on your income. Our mission is to bridge the gap between people and trusted legal resources through education, referrals, and community partnerships.
