Volunteer With Us

Justice Bridge Project is powered by people who believe everyone deserves access to legal information and trusted community resources.

🎓 Student Volunteers

College and law students can help with research, outreach, educational materials, and community events.

⚖️ Attorney Volunteers

Licensed attorneys are welcome to explore future opportunities to support legal education and community partnerships.

🤝 Community Volunteers

Help us organize events, connect with local organizations, and expand awareness of available legal resources.

Why Volunteer?

Volunteers are essential to our mission. Whether you are a student, attorney, or community member, your time and skills can help expand access to justice and strengthen communities.
