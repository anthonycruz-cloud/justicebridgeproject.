Our Services

Justice Bridge Project helps connect individuals with trusted legal resources and educational materials. We do not provide legal advice or legal representation.

⚖️ Legal Aid Referrals

Connect with nonprofit legal aid organizations and pro bono attorneys based on your needs.

📚 Know Your Rights

Learn about your rights through easy-to-understand educational resources.

🏠 Housing Resources

Find organizations that assist with landlord-tenant issues and housing concerns.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Resources

Locate community organizations and legal aid providers for family-related matters.

💼 Employment Resources

Access information and referrals for workplace rights and employment issues.

🤝 Community Workshops

Educational events designed to help communities better understand the legal system.
